\begin{corollary}[Dependency interpolation at fixed marginals]
\label{cor:depint}
Fix the hypergraph, labels, channel rate $p$, per-edge rule, and
linear operator. Assign each hyperedge independently and uniformly
to one of $K$ independent node-prediction copies, so that two
distinct edges share a copy with probability $\tau=1/K$ and the
per-edge error marginals are unchanged. Writing $\Delta B$ for the
resulting error operator,
\[
\mathbb E\,\|\Delta B-\mathbb E\,\Delta B\|_F^{2}
= V_0+\tau\,C_1,\qquad
V_0=\sum_e \widehat{ph}_e(1-\widehat{ph}_e),
\]
\[
C_1=\sum_{e\cap f\neq\emptyset}
\mathrm{Cov}_1(\delta_e,\delta_f)\,
\frac{|e\cap f|^{2}}{k_e k_f},
\]
where $\mathrm{Cov}_1$ is the covariance under a single shared
copy; disjoint pairs contribute exactly zero, so the entire
correlated excess enters through shared nodes. The endpoint
$\tau=0$ (independent per-edge errors) recovers model~B's law.
\end{corollary}

\paragraph{Verification.}
Across three synthetic seeds and the Cora and DBLP structures
(ten structure--rate combinations), the measured centered energy
follows the predicted line: regression of the six grouping points
($\tau\in\{1,\tfrac12,\dots,\tfrac1{32}\}$) gives intercepts and
slopes within Monte-Carlo error of the closed forms $V_0$ and
$C_1$ ($R^2=0.988$--$1.000$), and the fitted intercept at
$\tau\to0$ agrees with two independent $\tau=0$ estimates (direct
per-edge sampling and model~B's flip construction).
Figure~\ref{fig:depint}(left) shows the normalized excess
$(V_K-V_0)/C_1$ against $\tau$ with Monte-Carlo error bars; all
combinations collapse onto the line $y=\tau$. The centered
spectral norm (Figure~\ref{fig:depint}, right) decreases
monotonically in $\tau$ in every tested combination and
approaches the independent-channel level---an empirical
observation for which we offer no monotonicity derivation; the
affine law concerns second-order energy only.
